The balloon starts at rest, so the impulse from the launch equals its momentum at release. The ground must give the same size of impulse as the landing momentum to stop it.

Assumptions

  • No air resistance.
  • The bands follow Hooke's law, F = k·s.
  • The balloon leaves the pouch when the bands return to their rest length, at the release height.
  • Mass, stiffness and wobble are estimates until measured on the real launcher.

What each part of this page shows

  1. Projectile physics engine. The Kinematics card and the path plot update with every slider.
  2. Forces and energy tracker. Force arrows ride on the balloon in the 3D view. The energy bar follows elastic, kinetic and gravitational energy through the launch.
  3. Interactive 3D launcher. Rotate and zoom the model. Parts are labeled to match the blueprint.
  4. Animated modification. Pick an angle and the pin pulls out, the rail tilts and the pin locks. Step through Load, Release, Flight and Impact.
  5. Original against modified. The accuracy test fires 20 shots from each launcher at the same settings and compares where they land.
  6. Momentum and variables. The Momentum card, with sliders for mass, pullback, band stiffness and release height.
